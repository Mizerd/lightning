# Lightning 0.9.1

A fixing round. 0.9.0 was the largest feature release this client has had, and
0.9.1 is the week after it: the maintainer and the first outside users ran it
on real accounts, reported what was wrong, and this is the answer to that
list. Two things are new rather than fixed — a favourites section, and
notifications on Windows and macOS at all — and the rest is repair.

## Rooms load and scroll like they used to

0.9.0 made one kind of room slow to open and heavy to scroll: a room that
hosts calls. A MatrixRTC call re-publishes one membership state event per
participant per minute, so a room used for calls accumulates thousands of
them, and every one had become a hidden row in the timeline — paginated
twenty at a time, turned into a delegate, and counted by the loader that
decides how much history to fill on open. One room took five to ten seconds
to open while every other room was instant.

Those events are dropped at the source now, in the SDK's own event filter,
so they never become rows. Nothing on screen loses anything: the "started a
call" line is a different event and stays. Alongside it the history fill's
budget was cut back to the scale 0.8.3 used, it stops waiting on a retry
timer between pages that arrive instantly from the local cache, and it is
capped by row count as well as by page count, so a room whose recent history
is a long run of quiet activity no longer instantiates six hundred rows on
open. Pictures in the loaded history also wait until they are near the
viewport before they fetch, rather than all downloading the moment the room
opens.

## Notifications on Windows and macOS, and a read that clears them

On Windows and macOS, notifications did not appear at all — a build without
the Linux desktop-notification bus had one delivery path and no fallback, so
it showed nothing. Those platforms now deliver through the tray icon's
balloon: a toast in the Windows Action Centre, a user notification on macOS.
The icon shows whenever notifications are enabled, and clicking the balloon
opens the room it came from.

And reading a room now clears its notification from the desktop's history on
KDE and GNOME, where before an expired notification kept sitting there after
its room had been read.

## Favourites rise, and say so

A favourited room now rises to the top of its group in the Channels layout,
and Classic gets a **Favourites** section of its own between the invites and
the conversation feed — the shape Element uses. In the Channels row a star
sits beside the name: filled when the room is a favourite, an outline when
you hover, and a click toggles it.

## Home lists your direct messages too

The Channels Home view now lists your direct messages as a group under
Rooms, as well as in the People tab, so they are reachable from either place.
The People filter chip is offered at Home too.

## From the maintainer's testing

Everything below was reported on a real account and fixed:

- **Selecting messages works.** The selection circles fill as you select (the
  footer's count and the circles agree now), each sits in a column of its own
  instead of over the sender's avatar, and a date row no longer blocks the
  one beneath it. Leaving the room ends the selection.
- **"1 message selected" reads in the singular**, and "2 messages" in the
  plural.
- **The room title fits.** "Home" no longer reads "Ho…", and room names no
  longer carry a stray trailing ellipsis.
- **The message menu follows the message.** Opening a message's menu and then
  scrolling used to leave the menu floating in the middle of the window; it
  closes with the scroll now.
- **The mention popup closes when you have moved on** — after you have
  completed a mention and typed a space or two of the sentence after it,
  rather than following you through the rest of the line.
- **A screen share has a frame.** The picture sits inside a bordered box on
  the call stage and in the popout, instead of meeting the panel edge with no
  border, and the owner's nameplate fades after a few seconds so it does not
  cover the picture. The popout can be filled with a share by clicking its
  tile, and Escape brings the tiles back.
- **The call's hang-up button no longer clips** out of the "Voice connected"
  bar in a narrow window.
- **A call you are not allowed to join says so** on the status strip, instead
  of the Join button quietly doing nothing.
- **The profile card** has smooth banner corners, a thinner ring around the
  avatar, and its "away" tag sits beside the status dot.
- **A minimised window no longer pops the call out** on its own — the
  automatic floating window is opt-in now.
- **Two read receipts for one reader** are gone: a reader shows on one
  position.
- **The screen share on NixOS** works from a Nix-built client — the pipewire
  GStreamer plugin is on the plugin path now (a contributor's fix).
- **The tray crash on NixOS is fixed** (already in a 0.9.0 point rebuild): the
  process is a full application, so the tray works under bars that use the
  older XEmbed icon.

## New this round for diagnosing calls

A **changed display name colour** now reaches other people within about
twenty seconds, without either client restarting or the name having to
repaint first.

An opt-in **call statistics trace** (`LIGHTNING_CALL_STATS_TRACE=5`) logs
per-stream packet loss, jitter, bitrate and keyframe requests, for tracking
down a screen share that blurs and then fast-forwards. That symptom was
traced to the **sending** machine being at 100% CPU, which starves the
software video encoder; it is not fixed in this release.

## Known limitations

* **What was used for real, and what was not.** This release's GUI pass ran
  two test accounts against a real homeserver on Linux and confirmed: room
  loading in a call room, message selection, the room title, the message menu
  closing on scroll, the mention popup, the framed screen share both
  directions, the call popout, the "Voice connected" bar, the refused-join
  message, direct messages at Home, and the favourite star. Covered by
  automated tests only, not used against a real server or a second client
  this round: the Windows and macOS notification delivery, the KDE
  notification withdrawal on read, and the name-colour propagation timer.
* **A spotlighted screen share shows "Waiting for the picture" for a moment
  when you press "Back to grid"**, until you click the tile. The video is
  still arriving; only the grid tile has not re-attached. Recoverable and not
  new to this release.
* **The screen-share blur under load is not fixed** — see above; the cause is
  encoder starvation on the sending machine.
* **macOS remains untested** for everything, as in the last release. Windows
  was confirmed by a tester only for the camera running above 10 fps.
* **Switching themes still blocks the window for about a second**, as noted in
  0.9.0.
