# Lightning 0.9.9

A release made mostly of the interface: a sustained accessibility and layout
pass that turned dozens of "needs looking at" observations into fixed defects,
a Spaces rail you can now shape to taste, and a first-class NixOS package
contributed from outside the project. Nothing in the messaging, calling or
encryption contracts changed; this is the release that makes what was already
there legible, reachable and correctly drawn.

## Accessibility and layout

Several review rounds swept the interface with geometric checks rather than
source reading, and each one found collisions a source scan could not: two
affordances anchored to one edge that only overlap on a short row, ink drawn
the same colour as the fill beneath it, focus that could not reach a control
the eye could see. Cards that were the colour of the page on ten of eleven
themes now stand off it; a theme radio ring invisible in every light palette
is visible; the text selection highlight that reported "Ctrl+A doesn't work" is
drawn again; a destructive confirm that could not be clicked away can be. The
theme editor now grades the contrast of the colours you give it instead of
claiming a rating it never checked.

## The Spaces rail, shaped to taste

The Channels layout's Spaces rail draws the Space hierarchy as a tinted tree —
subspaces rearrange like any other tile, deep trees dive rather than pile up,
and depth carries a second cue beyond lightness. If you preferred the flat
list it used to be, that is now a setting: the rail can be the plain,
activity-ordered list again.

## Accounts, presence and the viewer

The account switcher is a clean list of rows, and every signed-in account's
last known avatar is kept on disk so it is there before the account syncs.
Presence publishing follows the server's own backoff and jitters its
keep-alive so two of your devices stop colliding. The image viewer gained
click-to-zoom, wheel-to-pan and navigation that wraps, and media and link
embeds can collapse to a single line in the timeline. A call now says out loud
when a peer is encrypting into a call the room believes is unencrypted.

## Packaging

**NixOS.** The Nix flake package gained webp and JPEG XL image support
(`qt6.qtimageformats` and `kdePackages.kimageformats`) and resolves the
`qt.multimedia` PipeWire symbol load by putting PipeWire on the wrapper's
library path; the flake inputs were refreshed to a nixpkgs that no longer
SIGSEGVs after 0.9.0. Contributed by **Seikm** and verified on NixOS with
`lightning-matrix --image-format-status`.

**Flathub.** The Flatpak submission manifest was rewritten to the form Flathub
asks for: the `cmake-ninja` buildsystem, no manifest-side file renames — the
desktop, icon and metainfo files are installed under the app id by the build
itself now — and no build restricted to a single architecture, so the aarch64
builder is free to try.
