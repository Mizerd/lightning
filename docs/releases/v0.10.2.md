# Lightning 0.10.2

A release about getting your conversations and settings out of the app and
back in: export a room as a web page with its photos, export your room keys,
take your settings to another device, and find an attachment by its name. It
also brings a rebuilt theme editor, read receipts as faces, a notification
troubleshooter, and updates that now come from GitHub first.

## Rooms and messages

- **Export a room as one web page**, with its photos, videos and files
  embedded, or as text or JSON. Choose the latest messages, the whole history
  or a date range, and a size limit for attachments. The page makes no network
  requests and runs no script. In an encrypted room, nothing private is written
  unless you tick the box that says so.
- **Read receipts are faces** under the message, with a "+N" pill and a "Seen
  by" list that grows as people read. Hovering a face lifts it; a pointer on its
  lower edge no longer makes it flicker.
- **The media browser** has GIF and Sticker tabs, a date range, and shows an
  edited message once, as its latest version.
- **Search finds an attachment by its file name**, also in encrypted rooms, and
  only the original sender's edit can change what a message says.
- **Invite people…** is on a room's right-click menu.
- A room unread only because of a thread says so.
- Mark as read marks the newest message even when it is outside what is loaded.
- A parked send queue recovers without a restart.

## Appearance

- **A rebuilt theme editor**: Simple, Groups and Elements levels with linked
  colours, a theme menu in the top bar, sharing and importing themes with a
  preview, a readability report with one-click fixes, named undo steps, and
  keyboard navigation of the preview.
- **Chat backgrounds can be animated** GIF or WebP, following your GIF
  autoplay and reduced-motion settings. Very fast flashing animations are kept
  still.
- The Spaces rail can be ordered A–Z or by recent activity, and rooms by
  unread first.
- The profile card opens beside what you clicked.

## Security and privacy

- **Export your room keys** to a file Element can import, behind a passphrase.
  The strength meter no longer rates a repeated or common password as strong.
- **A message that cannot be decrypted fetches its key** from your key backup
  or your own verified devices, without a restart.
- **Photos lose their location when you send them**, in threads too, and more
  camera metadata is removed.
- Signing out warns you when your keys are not backed up.
- Media previews have their own settings.

## Settings

- **Export and import a settings file**, and optionally sync settings between
  your devices. The import preview and the result now count the same thing.
- Settings are grouped, with a page outline, a search, and a link you can copy
  to any setting.
- **The interface follows a language change everywhere**, including keyboard
  shortcut names and the connection status, and numbers and sizes use the
  language you chose rather than your desktop's.
- About has Copy version, your homeserver's details, **Reset local cache**
  (which now gives the disk space back), and developer tools.
- Screen-share and camera quality settings, with Auto, and echo and gain
  switches.

## Notifications

- **Your account's notification rules** in Settings: keywords (with a confirm
  before removing one), per-room defaults, and a troubleshooter that checks why
  a notification did not arrive.

## Help and diagnostics

- **Export a log bundle** to attach to a report. It contains no message text,
  tokens, IDs or paths, and the diagnostic log stays off until you turn it on.

## Updates and installs

- **Updates are read from GitHub first**, with GitLab as the fallback. The
  manifest is still signed and checked the same way.
- Throwaway caches live in their own `cache/` folder beside your account data,
  and are moved there on first start.
- **Upgrading with the Windows MSI keeps every file.** Since the MSI was
  introduced, an upgrade could report success and leave Lightning without
  Lightning.exe and its libraries. If an earlier MSI upgrade left Lightning
  unable to start, install the 0.10.2 MSI and it will be complete again.
- The AppImage carries the sound mixer that per-app share audio needs (#37).
- macOS: restoring from the menu bar no longer crashes, closing the window hides
  it, and Cmd+W closes it as in other apps (#34).
- Tapping with a finger no longer opens right-click menus (thanks, Tom).

## Calls

- Media keys are 16 bytes, as Element and Sable use.

## Known limitations

- macOS is NOT TESTED live in this release.
- In the media browser, search by caption was reported missing once in testing
  and could not be reproduced; please report it if you see it.
- An exported web page embeds media as data: URLs; some browsers will not play a
  very large embedded video.
- Windows packages remain unsigned.
