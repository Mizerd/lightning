# Lightning 0.9.3

A release about things that failed quietly. Most of what follows was already
broken in 0.9.2 and gave you nothing to go on: a call that was silent in one
direction while the other person heard you perfectly, admin controls that
simply were not there, a report you submitted that never said whether it
arrived, settings that changed when you switched accounts and told nobody.
Each of those now either works or says what happened.

## A session that can never decrypt anything now tells you

This is the important one.

A device can end up publishing an encryption key that its own account does
not hold. When that happens nothing addressed to that device can ever be
opened: encrypted messages sit on "Waiting for keys" forever, and in a call
you can be heard perfectly while hearing nobody, because sending is
unaffected. It does not recover on its own, and rejoining does not help.

Until now Lightning showed you "Waiting for keys" and nothing else. It now
detects the condition, explains it in plain language, and offers the one
repair that works: signing out and signing in again. The confirmation states
the cost honestly, and it names your situation specifically rather than
hedging: if key backup is not set up on the account, it says so outright,
because encrypted messages already on that computer will not come back.

Nothing is automated and nothing is deleted behind your back. The repair is
the ordinary sign-out, and you choose it.

Related: a call media key that cannot be opened is no longer discarded in
silence, and a to-device message that fails to decrypt is now recorded rather
than vanishing. That last one is why this took so long to find; the failure
left no trace anywhere.

## Calls

- **A refused call now says which gate refused it.** "You don't have
  permission to join this call" was shown both when the room's permissions
  blocked you and when the calling service refused the connection. Those are
  different problems with different fixes, and they now read differently.
- **And the refusal stops outliving the join that succeeded.** Pressing Join
  again and getting in used to leave the old error on screen, so you sat in a
  call being told you could not be in it.
- **Someone who joins a call already in progress is no longer nameless and
  faceless** to everyone else in it.
- **A participant you had deafened could still be heard** if their audio
  arrived while deafened. Fixed.
- **Screen sharing no longer sends the call's own audio back into the call**,
  on Windows as well as Linux, so nobody hears themselves. An application
  that starts playing *during* a share is now captured too, where previously
  only whatever was already playing got picked up.
- **Video volume is adjustable without expanding the player.**
- A recipient's device list is refreshed before a media key is encrypted to
  them, and a failed key distribution now actually retries.

## Spaces

- A subspace that sits under two Spaces is now listed under both. Previously
  one parent counted its rooms on the badge and then showed you an empty
  column.
- The Space you have selected now leads its own view instead of appearing
  below its subspaces.
- **A Space you are in keeps its place in the rail even when you have not
  joined any of its rooms.** Creating a Space, or joining one from Explore,
  used to throw you back to Home the moment anything refreshed, which is
  exactly when you wanted the Space open.
- Renaming a Space, or changing its picture, now reaches an open Space
  settings window instead of leaving it frozen on what it showed when opened.

## The AppImage has its icon back

The AppImage showed a generic placeholder in the task bar and window switcher
after updating to 0.9.1 or 0.9.2.

The cause was not the icon. Qt's Wayland support has no way to hand a window
an icon at all, so the only route left is a launcher entry the desktop can
find, and an AppImage installs none. Earlier versions had been getting away
with it by running under XWayland, where a different mechanism applies; a
0.9.1 fix moved the client onto native Wayland and took the icon with it.

An AppImage now publishes its own launcher entry and icons on first run. It
never overwrites an entry or an icon you put there yourself, and it steps
aside for an installed package rather than shadowing it. Set
`LIGHTNING_NO_DESKTOP_INTEGRATION=1` to turn it off.

## Calls, continued: the padlock told you the call was fine

A participant whose audio and video were being thrown away sat under a
**green padlock**. The call was encrypted, which is what the badge measured,
and it was not the whole answer: the media engine has always detected a
remote stream whose frames arrive and cannot be decrypted, and only ever
wrote it to a log nobody reads.

The badge now changes when that happens and says which case it is on hover.
Encrypted still means encrypted; it just stops being reassuring while
somebody's media is being dropped.

## Destructive actions ask first

Six of them acted the moment you clicked, while every reversible action
beside them asked: Delete, Remove edits, End poll, Set role, Remove widget
and Ignore user.

The sharpest is the role change, and it is now the most explicit. Matrix
refuses a power-level change at or above your own, so **promoting somebody to
your own level cannot be undone**, and the confirmation says exactly that
when it applies.

Ignoring someone is confirmed because it is account-wide, on every device,
and hides messages they have already sent. Un-ignoring is harmless and asks
nothing.

## Moderation and administration

- **Admin controls were invisible on the ordinary path.** Opening someone's
  card from their avatar in the timeline, from a mention, or from "View
  profile" showed no Kick, Ban or role controls at all unless you had
  happened to open Room Information for that room earlier in the session.
- **A report you submit now tells you what the server did with it.** It used
  to close the dialog immediately and never say whether the report was
  accepted, refused, rate limited or lost.
- **Favourite, Mark as read and Mark as unread now report a refusal.** When
  the server said no, the menu simply did not change, which is
  indistinguishable from a slow connection.
- A refused "Stop ignoring" is no longer silent.

## Notifications and settings

- Three notifications that outlived what raised them are fixed, including one
  that could still open the previous account's room after a switch, and one
  that appeared moments after you had already read the room.
- **Five settings changed when you switched accounts and told nothing.** The
  worst is reduced motion, an accessibility choice that was leaking between
  accounts; the others are the clock format, smooth scrolling, which composer
  buttons exist, and microphone gain, which is applied to a live call.
- Turning off "keep running in the tray" now correctly unticks "start in the
  tray", which had stayed ticked for a setting that read false.

## Messages and the timeline

- **A numbered list keeps its numbers** instead of restarting at 1 when the
  message is drawn again.
- Replying no longer claims replies are unimplemented, and no longer wedges
  the composer afterwards.
- Stickers animate, decided by the file itself rather than by a label senders
  often omit.
- The Media tab opens the picture you clicked, and its tiles no longer crop
  the subject out of a tall screenshot or a wide panorama. Nearly-square
  pictures still fill their tile so the grid stays even; anything further off
  square is shown whole.
- **Stickers animate in the picker you choose them from**, not only once they
  are sent.
- A notification for a **thread reply** now takes you to the thread instead of
  landing on "message unavailable". It was asking the room timeline to find
  an event that timeline deliberately hides.
- **A Space's header shows its name.** A Space you had just created was
  labelled "Empty Room" until the next restart.
- Scrolling towards the top of a room loads a bounded amount rather than
  running away.
- **Bridge tags now come from what the bridge itself advertises**, so a
  bridged group is labelled and not just a bridged direct message. What is
  learned is remembered between restarts, so the labels are there when you
  open the app instead of appearing only after you visit each chat.

## Updating

Automatic updates work for the Debian package, the RPM, the AppImage and all
three Windows formats. Flatpak and Snap are updated by their own stores, and
Lightning tells you a version exists rather than pretending it can install
it. macOS is download-only and is deliberately absent from the update
manifest.

As always, and it is worth repeating: **an update cannot fix the updater
already on your disk.** Fixes to the update mechanism itself only take effect
from the version that carries them onward, so anyone on a build with a broken
updater has to install once by hand.

## Known and not fixed

An attached **audio** file still arrives without its duration, so the card
reads "0:00" beside a correct size. Video is unaffected. Supplying it means
carrying a duration across the Rust boundary, which is a change to the
send path rather than a small fix, and doing that on release day is not a
trade worth making for a badge. The two shortcuts available would either be
a semantic lie or would mark every attached music file as a voice message.

The encryption fault above is **detected and explained, not repaired
automatically**. The repair is a manual sign-out and sign-in, and that is
deliberate: anything more would mean deleting local data on your behalf.

That detection has not yet been seen firing on a genuinely broken device,
because none was available to test against. Its logic is covered by tests in
both directions, including the one that matters most: a check that cannot
reach an answer is treated as "unknown" and never as "broken", so being
offline never tells you your encryption is destroyed.
