# Lightning 0.10.6

A features and fixes release. Chats can be archived, the header shows whether a
room is encrypted or public, Ctrl+Tab cycles rooms and Ctrl+` switches
accounts, profiles show a person's local time and pronouns, encrypted calls
receive VP9 video, the image viewer zooms with the mouse wheel on KDE Wayland,
and several default shortcuts moved off keys that input methods and desktops
already use. Thanks to LTSCommerce for their contribution.

## New

- **Archive chats.** Archive or Unarchive a room from its menu or the batch
  bar. It stays joined and keeps notifying, but leaves the other filters,
  Spaces and badge totals and is listed under an "Archived" chip (a button in
  the compact list). Archiving is stored as a room tag, so it follows you to
  your other sessions of Lightning.
- **Encryption and public state in the header.** The room header shows a lock
  for an encrypted room, a struck-through lock for an unencrypted one and a
  globe for a public one. Public rooms also carry a small globe on their
  avatar in the room list.
- **Cycle rooms with Ctrl+Tab and Ctrl+Shift+Tab**, in the order the room list
  shows them (current filter, Space and layout), wrapping at both ends and
  skipping invitations. They work from the message box too.
- **Switch accounts with Ctrl+` and Ctrl+Shift+`**, in saved order, skipping
  accounts that need signing in again.
- **Next and previous unread room** on Alt+Shift+Down and Alt+Shift+Up.
- **Time zone and pronouns on profiles.** A profile card shows the person's
  local time and pronouns, and Settings has a time zone picker and a pronouns
  field. Name and card colours set in Sable are shown too; "Show profile
  colours from other users" in Appearance turns them off.
- **Hide the online status dots.** Settings › Privacy & security › Presence:
  Automatic (the new default) hides them on servers that never share anyone's
  status, such as matrix.org, where everyone would otherwise look offline.
- **Search matrixrooms.info from Explore** (#73). The directory picker lists
  your own server, matrixrooms.info and a server you type. It is never the
  default, the request goes through your own homeserver, and a note says your
  search terms reach a third party while it is selected.
- **VP9 video in encrypted calls** (#72). Lightning now receives VP9 from
  clients that publish it without a VP8 fallback, where before the tile waited
  for ever. A track in a codec Lightning cannot decode now says so on its tile.
- **Choose where Bubbles applies**: direct messages, direct messages and
  private rooms, or all rooms (Settings › Layout).
- **Zoom the image viewer with the mouse wheel**, in and out at the pointer,
  including on KDE Plasma Wayland, where the wheel did nothing before. A
  touchpad pans a zoomed picture and pinches to zoom, and the thumbnail strip
  steps one picture per wheel notch.
- **Open a room, Space or person's picture full size** from the avatar in the
  header.
- **Copy a range of messages** as plain text with sender and time (#78).
  Shift+J and Shift+K, or a Shift-click, extend the keyboard selection into a
  range, and Shift+C or the menu's Copy messages copies it, in the timeline
  and in a thread.
- **Your pending attachments wait for you.** Switching rooms or threads no
  longer clears the attachments queued in the message box; they come back
  when you return.
- **Open an inviter's profile from the invitation**, and see how many rooms
  you already share with them.
- **Custom emoji animate** in reactions, the emoji picker and completion,
  following the autoplay setting.
- **Search indexes older messages in the background** after you sign in
  (Privacy & security, on by default), and new messages as they arrive, so
  local search finds more without asking. An empty search says when older
  messages were last indexed and offers to index them.
- **matrix: links open in Lightning.** The Linux launchers handle `matrix:`
  links, a link passed on the command line opens through the join flow, and a
  second launch hands it to the running Lightning. `matrix:` links inside
  messages stay links, and a link to a person becomes a mention pill.
- **Hide the message action bar on hover**, under Settings › Chat ›
  Timeline. The right-click menu and keyboard actions stay.
- **React from the thread panel** with A on the selected message, as in the
  timeline.
- **A long thread root scrolls** in the thread panel instead of being cut off
  after six lines (#75). Contributed by LTSCommerce.
- **A long bio scrolls** in the profile card, and a long status shows in full
  on hover.
- The side panel can grow wider than 640 px on a wide window (#76), and the
  Threads list takes the whole panel when no thread is open (#79).
- Text shows the I-beam cursor and message links the pointing hand.
- A shortcut key that another action holds only by default can now be taken,
  with a warning naming that action.

## Changed default shortcuts

Several defaults collided with input methods (IBus takes Ctrl+Shift+U),
KWin or common paste keys. Only the defaults moved: **a shortcut you changed
yourself is left exactly as you set it.**

| Action | 0.10.5 | 0.10.6 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mute or unmute the microphone | Ctrl+Shift+U | Ctrl+Shift+M |
| Deafen or undeafen | Ctrl+Shift+H | Ctrl+Shift+D |
| Turn the camera on or off | Ctrl+Shift+V | **unbound** |
| Mark the open conversation as read | Ctrl+Shift+M | Ctrl+R |
| Start a direct message | Ctrl+Shift+T | Ctrl+N |
| Show or hide Stream Info | Ctrl+F9 | Ctrl+Shift+F9 |
| Next / previous conversation | — | Ctrl+Tab / Ctrl+Shift+Tab |
| Next / previous unread conversation | — | Alt+Shift+Down / Alt+Shift+Up |
| Next / previous account | — | Ctrl+\` / Ctrl+Shift+\` |
| Back / forward (macOS) | Option+Left / Option+Right | Cmd+[ / Cmd+] |
| Next / previous conversation (macOS) | — | Control+Tab / Control+Shift+Tab |

The camera has no default key any more; bind one in Settings › Shortcuts if
you want it.

## Memory and speed

- **A room left open no longer grows without limit.** Every new message in
  the open room stayed loaded until you left it (measured at about 0.55 MB
  each). After two minutes away from the app with the room at its newest
  message, Lightning trims it back to the latest history. It never does this
  while you are reading, typing, watching media or have a menu or viewer
  open.
- **A message waiting for its keys no longer burns CPU.** Its placeholder
  animated for as long as the room stayed open, which could keep several
  cores busy on a software renderer. The animation now stops after 15 seconds
  and the placeholder stays drawn.

## Fixes

- **Ignoring or unignoring someone no longer blanks the room list.** Every
  room's preview and its place in the list were lost; they are now kept.
- Opening a room no longer blanks its preview or moves it in the list because
  of a profile change or another state event.
- A renamed person shows their current name in the timeline, search results,
  reply quotes, thread summaries, invitations and the profile card.
- "Today", "Yesterday" and day dividers update when the day changes, also
  after suspend and a time zone change.
- A reply to a message the timeline had not loaded no longer stays at
  "(original message not loaded)".
- **Jumping to an older message**: right-clicking a message there acted on the
  message underneath it in the timeline. The view's own messages now have
  their actions, your own can be edited, and the message box stays available.
- Pictures no longer go missing for a minute after switching accounts or
  while a session is being restored.
- Avatars load on servers that do not make thumbnails.
- Room information no longer loops its layout at some widths.
- Settings loads your saved bio before showing it, and Save stays off until
  you change it, so saving can no longer erase a bio it never loaded.
- The thread list keeps its order when more threads load, and its reply counts
  no longer grow after visiting a thread.
- The "New messages" divider no longer sits at the bottom with nothing under
  it.
- A locked GNOME keyring now asks to be unlocked once at startup instead of
  reading as signed out. A keyring that refuses to store a sign-in no longer
  changes an account already saved on this device.
- The AppImage keeps a launcher entry or icon you edited instead of rewriting
  it at every start (#71), and its window now matches its launcher.
- The compact room list has the room menu on right-click.
- A room offers only the mark-read or mark-unread action that applies, and
  your own message's menu puts Edit beside Reply.
- The room list's filter arrows no longer cover a chip.
- A search match on a highlighted row is readable on every theme.
- A Settings section's name is centred on its icon.
- Translations were resynced; new strings are in English until translated.

## Updates

- The updater tries GitHub first, then gitlab.com, then Lightning's own
  server. A bad file or manifest from either mirror moves on to the next
  source; every file is still checked against the signed manifest, and the
  status line says in plain words which server the update came from.

## Project

- The GitHub repository moved to
  [lightning-matrix/lightning](https://github.com/lightning-matrix/lightning).
  Links in the app, the docs and the package metadata point there; the old
  address redirects.
- The project now has a security policy (`SECURITY.md`).

## Known limits and not yet tested

- **A reopened jump-to-message view can show stale edits or deletions** until
  Lightning is restarted (a limit of matrix-sdk 0.18).
- **The first sign-in writes more to disk for a few minutes** while older
  messages are indexed for search: about 80 MB in the first three minutes on
  the test account, against about 1 MB for 0.10.5. Turn off "Index older
  messages in the background" to avoid it. Steady idle use is unchanged.
- **VP9 receive is NOT TESTED against a live VP9 sender**; it is covered by
  automated tests only. AV1 is not received in encrypted calls.
- **macOS is NOT TESTED**, and so are the snap, `.deb` and `.rpm` packages'
  interfaces (they pass the automated package checks) and the camera.
- **Audio from Windows to Linux in a call was heard, but its continuity is NOT
  TESTED**: the test rig's remote-desktop microphone delivers audio in bursts
  on its own.
- **"Media from someone here cannot be decrypted" in a call still has no
  automatic recovery.** Leaving and rejoining the call may help.
- Windows packages are unsigned.

## How this release was checked

Automated tests (Rust and C++ suites, on gitlab.com runners) and two manual
passes on development builds made a few hours before this release:

- **NixOS, PASS:** Ctrl+Tab and Ctrl+Shift+Tab, Alt+Shift+Up and Down,
  Ctrl+R and Ctrl+Shift+R, Ctrl+` and Ctrl+Shift+` with two accounts,
  archiving in both layouts and the compact list, the empty Archived list,
  ignore and unignore, `matrix:` person and room links, the jump view with
  the message box, saving a bio, the shortcut overlap warning and reset, image
  viewer wheel zoom, and the layouts checked for overlaps in light and dark
  themes at three window widths and at 115% text size. Memory at or below
  0.10.5.
- **Windows 11, the Flatpak and the AppImage, PASS:** start, sign-in and
  encrypted chat both ways; calls Flatpak to AppImage and AppImage to Windows
  with audio both ways (measured with a test tone) and screen sharing both
  ways; Ctrl+Tab, the header icons, archiving (synced between devices) and
  image viewer zoom on all three; wheel zoom on KDE Plasma 6.7 Wayland in a
  virtual machine (with an emulated pointer, not a physical mouse). Ten
  minutes idle in a call: steady memory and under 2.5 MB written.
- **NOT TESTED:** macOS; the snap, `.deb` and `.rpm` interfaces; Windows to
  Linux audio continuity (above); the camera.
